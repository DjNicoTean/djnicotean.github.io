About

30 years behind the decks

NicoTean is a German electronic music DJ with three decades of experience behind the decks. Active since 1996, his journey has taken him from Germany’s underground electronic music scene through clubs and festivals across Europe to Malta and Southeast Asia.

Over the years, he has performed at venues and events including Love Parade Berlin, Harry Klein Munich, Tunnel Hamburg, Warehouse Cologne, Fusion Club Münster, RoXY and Studio 80 Amsterdam, La Rocca Belgium and Rohstofflager Zürich. His wider European journey also includes appearances in London, Malta and Switzerland.

Beyond DJing, NicoTean founded Deichrocker Bremen and later Team Orange Malta. In Malta, he developed Berlin Big Beats into an eleven edition event series at Funky Monkey and was involved in projects including Bunker Rave and BeatBoatBerlin.

Across his career, NicoTean has shared line ups with artists including Robert Babicz, Guy J, Markus Carp, Lil Lewis, Pig & Dan, Monika Kruse, Oliver Huntemann, Steve Bug, UMEK, Chris Liebing, Stephan Bodzin, Thomas Schumacher, Oliver Schories and Pier Bucci.

Since 2022, Thailand has become a recurring chapter in NicoTean’s journey, with four consecutive winter seasons and performances across Phuket, Krabi, Koh Lanta, Southern Thailand and Bangkok, bringing his sound to clubs, bars and open air settings across the country.

Today, NicoTean’s sound moves between Deep House, Progressive House and Deep Techno, combining hypnotic grooves, melodic progression and a strong sense of flow, shaped by three decades behind the decks and an instinctive understanding of the dancefloor.
