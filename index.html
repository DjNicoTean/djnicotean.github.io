About

30 years behind the decks

NicoTean is a German electronic music DJ with three decades of experience behind the decks. Active since 1996, his journey has taken him from Germany’s underground electronic music scene through clubs and festivals across Europe to Malta and Southeast Asia.

Over the years, he has performed at venues and events including Love Parade Berlin, Harry Klein Munich, Tunnel Hamburg, Warehouse Cologne, Fusion Club Münster, Roxy and Studio 80 Amsterdam, La Rocca Belgium and Rohstofflager Zürich. His wider European journey also includes appearances in London, Malta and Switzerland.

Beyond DJing, NicoTean co founded Deichrocker Bremen and later founded Team Orange Malta, developing independent electronic music concepts and events including Berlin Big Beats, Bunker Rave and BeatBoatBerlin. Across his career he has shared line ups with artists including Robert Babicz, Guy J, Oliver Huntemann, Steve Bug, Stephan Bodzin, Oliver Schories and Pier Bucci.

Since 2022, Thailand has become another recurring chapter, with four consecutive winter seasons and performances across Koh Lanta, Southern Thailand and Bangkok.

Today, NicoTean’s sound moves between Deep House, Progressive House and Deep Techno, combining hypnotic grooves, melodic progression and a strong sense of flow shaped by three decades of dancefloor experience.
