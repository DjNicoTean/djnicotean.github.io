About

30 years behind the decks

NicoTean is a German electronic music DJ with three decades of experience behind the decks. Active since 1996, his journey began in Germany’s underground electronic music scene and developed through clubs, festivals and open air events across Europe, Malta and Southeast Asia.

Over the years, he has performed at venues and events including Love Parade Berlin, Berghain, Bar 25, Club der Visionäre, Kater Blau, Harry Klein Munich, Tunnel Hamburg, Fusion Club Münster, Studio 80 Amsterdam, Rohstofflager Zürich and Zero Club London.

Beyond individual performances, NicoTean co founded Deichrocker Bremen and Team Orange Malta. In Malta, Berlin Big Beats developed into an eleven edition event series at Funky Monkey, alongside projects including Bunker Rave and BeatBoatBerlin.

Across his career, he has shared line ups with artists including Robert Babicz, Guy J, Oliver Huntemann, Steve Bug, UMEK, Chris Liebing, Monika Kruse, Stephan Bodzin, Thomas Schumacher, Oliver Schories and Pier Bucci.

Since 2022, Thailand has become a recurring chapter in his journey, with four consecutive winter seasons and performances across Phuket, Krabi, Koh Lanta, Southern Thailand and Bangkok.

Today, NicoTean’s sound moves between Deep House, Progressive House and Deep Techno, combining hypnotic grooves, melodic progression and a strong sense of flow shaped by three decades of dancefloor experience.

The Journey

From Germany to Malta and Southeast Asia

NicoTean’s journey behind the decks began in Germany in 1996. What started in the German underground electronic music scene developed into club, festival and open air appearances across Europe, including Love Parade Berlin, Berghain Berlin, Bar 25 Berlin, Club der Visionäre Berlin, Kater Blau Berlin, Harry Klein Munich, Tunnel Hamburg and Fusion Club Münster.

The journey continued beyond Germany with performances at Warehouse Cologne, Studio 80 Amsterdam, Rohstofflager Zürich and Zero Club London. Music was never only about playing a set and leaving again. The culture around the music, the people and the events became just as important. In Bremen, NicoTean co founded Deichrocker Bremen, while BexxTownBeats Radio became another part of that chapter.

After moving to Malta, a new chapter developed around the local electronic music scene. As co founder of Team Orange Malta, NicoTean helped establish Berlin Big Beats, which grew into an eleven edition event series at Funky Monkey Malta. Other projects and appearances followed, including Bunker Rave Malta, BeatBoatBerlin and Cafe Society Valletta.

Since 2022, Southeast Asia has become another regular part of the journey. Four consecutive winter seasons in Thailand have included performances around Phuket, Krabi, Koh Lanta and Bangkok, with sets at Ozone Beach Bar Koh Lanta, Lighthouse Bar Koh Lanta, Sanctuary Koh Lanta, Fusion Bar Koh Lanta and 420 Rooftop Bangkok.

Across three decades, NicoTean has shared line ups with artists from different generations of electronic music, including Robert Babicz, Guy J, Oliver Huntemann, Steve Bug, UMEK, Chris Liebing, Monika Kruse, Stephan Bodzin, Thomas Schumacher, Oliver Schories, Pier Bucci, Pig & Dan, WestBam, Dr. Motte, Timo Maas and Mark Spoon.

The locations have changed over the years, but the connection to underground electronic music has remained. Today NicoTean’s sets move naturally between Deep House, Progressive House and Deep Techno, shaped by experience, musical instinct and the dancefloor rather than trends.
