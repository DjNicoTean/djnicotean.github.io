About

30 years behind the decks

NicoTean is a German electronic music DJ with three decades of experience behind the decks. Active since 1996, his journey began in Germany’s underground electronic music scene and developed through clubs, festivals and open air events across Europe, Malta and Southeast Asia.

Over the years, he has performed at venues and events including Love Parade Berlin, Berghain, Bar 25, Club der Visionäre, Kater Blau, Harry Klein Munich, Tunnel Hamburg, Warehouse Cologne, Fusion Club Münster, RoXY and Studio 80 Amsterdam, Rohstofflager Zürich and Zero Club London.

Beyond individual performances, NicoTean co-founded Deichrocker Bremen and Team Orange Malta. In Malta, Berlin Big Beats developed into an eleven-edition event series, while Bunker Rave and BeatBoatBerlin became part of his resident DJ history.

Across his career, he has shared line ups with artists including Robert Babicz, Guy J, Markus Carp, Lil Lewis, Pig & Dan, Monika Kruse, Oliver Huntemann, Steve Bug, UMEK, Chris Liebing, Stephan Bodzin, Thomas Schumacher, Oliver Schories and Pier Bucci.

Since 2022, Thailand has become another recurring chapter, with four consecutive winter seasons and performances across Phuket, Krabi, Koh Lanta, Southern Thailand and Bangkok.

Today, NicoTean’s sound moves between Deep House, Progressive House and Deep Techno, combining hypnotic grooves, melodic progression and a strong sense of flow shaped by three decades of dancefloor experience.
