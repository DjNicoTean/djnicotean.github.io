The Journey

From Germany to Malta and Southeast Asia

NicoTean’s journey behind the decks began in Germany in 1996, at a time when electronic music was moving out of the clubs and into a much bigger underground culture. What started in the German scene soon led to appearances at clubs, festivals and open air events, including Love Parade Berlin, Berghain Berlin, Bar 25 Berlin, Club der Visionäre Berlin, Kater Blau Berlin, Harry Klein Munich, Tunnel Hamburg and Fusion Club Münster.

The journey continued beyond Germany with performances at Warehouse Cologne, Studio 80 Amsterdam, Rohstofflager Zürich and Zero Club London. Along the way, NicoTean was never interested only in turning up for a set and leaving again. Music, events and the people around them have always been part of the same story. In Bremen he co founded Deichrocker Bremen, while radio became another part of that journey through BexxTownBeats Radio.

After moving to Malta, a new chapter developed around the local electronic music scene. As co founder of Team Orange Malta, NicoTean helped establish Berlin Big Beats, which grew into an eleven edition event series at Funky Monkey Malta. Other projects and appearances followed, including Bunker Rave Malta, BeatBoatBerlin and, more recently, Cafe Society Valletta.

Since 2022, Southeast Asia has become another regular part of the journey. Four consecutive winter seasons in Thailand have included performances around Phuket, Krabi, Koh Lanta and Bangkok, with sets at Ozone Beach Bar Koh Lanta, Lighthouse Bar Koh Lanta, Sanctuary Koh Lanta, Fusion Bar Koh Lanta and 420 Rooftop Bangkok.

Across those three decades, NicoTean has shared line ups with artists from different generations of electronic music, including Robert Babicz, Guy J, Oliver Huntemann, Steve Bug, UMEK, Chris Liebing, Monika Kruse, Stephan Bodzin, Thomas Schumacher, Oliver Schories, Pier Bucci, Pig & Dan, WestBam, Dr. Motte, Timo Maas and Mark Spoon.

The locations have changed over the years, but the idea behind it has remained much the same. NicoTean has stayed connected to underground electronic music from the German scene of the 1990s, through more than a decade in Malta and into his recent seasons in Southeast Asia. Today his sets move naturally between Deep House, Progressive House and Deep Techno, shaped by experience rather than trends and always with the dancefloor at the centre of it.
